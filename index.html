Privacy Policy

ADEPA TEACHERS’ CO-OPERATIVE CREDIT UNION LTD
ADEPA TEACHERS mobile application · Version 1.0
Effective date: 9 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ADEPA Teachers’ Co-operative Credit Union Ltd (“ADEPA”, “we”, “us” or “our”) handles personal information when you use the ADEPA TEACHERS mobile application (“App”).

1. Information handled by the App

Depending on the features you use, the App may handle information you provide, such as your name, phone number, email address, member or account identifiers, registration details and information submitted for identity verification. It may also display or send account balances, savings, loan and share details, applications, transfers, payment instructions, transaction records and related references. Authentication information, including PINs, passwords, one-time codes and session tokens, is used to verify and secure access. The App also creates an installation identifier and may send device details such as platform, model, manufacturer and operating-system version for device recognition and security.

2. How information is used

ADEPA uses information to register and identify members, authenticate users and devices, provide account and financial services, display account and transaction information, process or verify service requests and payments, protect accounts, respond to support requests, maintain business records and meet legal or regulatory obligations.

3. Where information is sent and who may receive it

The App communicates with ADEPA’s online services hosted at adepaye.com to authenticate users and provide financial-service features. Information needed to complete a payment or other requested service may be shared with the relevant payment, mobile-money, banking or other service provider. ADEPA may also disclose information to its authorized service providers, professional advisers, regulators or other parties when required or permitted by law. Their handling of information is subject to their own applicable terms and privacy practices.

4. Storage and security

Some settings and app preferences are stored on your device. The App uses the device’s secure storage facilities for authentication secrets and sends requests to ADEPA services over HTTPS. Information held by ADEPA’s services is subject to the safeguards and access controls maintained by ADEPA and its service providers. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Keep your device and credentials secure and contact ADEPA promptly if you suspect unauthorized access.

5. Retention

ADEPA retains information for as long as it is needed to provide services, protect accounts, resolve disputes and meet applicable financial-recordkeeping and legal obligations. Retention periods depend on the type of information and the applicable requirements. Removing the App from your device does not delete records held by ADEPA or its service providers.

6. Your choices and privacy requests

You may contact ADEPA to ask about personal information associated with your account, request correction of inaccurate information or raise a privacy concern. Requests may require identity verification and may be limited where ADEPA must retain records or comply with law. Account closure or deletion requests should be sent to ADEPA using the contact details below; uninstalling the App does not close your account.

7. Legal basis and applicable law

ADEPA handles personal information for the purposes described in this Policy and in accordance with applicable laws, including Ghana’s Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843), where applicable.

8. Changes to this Policy

ADEPA may update this Policy when the App, services or applicable requirements change. The latest version and its effective date will be made available through ADEPA’s official channels. Please review it periodically.
